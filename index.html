Post Apocalyptic Space Cruise

Cruise between star systems and discover gas giants, rocky worlds, moons, and otherworldly surprises. Gravity affects your ship's speed: you'll automatically slow down when approaching celestial bodies and speed up again when travelling between stars.

Fun fact: This entire galaxy is generated in your browser by a single 28 KB HTML file!

Controls:

Mouse or drag to steer · W/S throttle · +/- or wheel: speed gear · Q/E roll · M mute · ESC quit
